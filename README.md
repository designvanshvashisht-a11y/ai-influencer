# AI Influencer Feasibility Research

Research into legal, platform-policy and payment feasibility for an **India-based operator of a fictional, clearly adult AI influencer**. The completed baseline audit covers an Instagram funnel to paid explicit AI content; a new conversation baseline records a proposed Instagram-native, non-nude subscription model.

**As of 17 October 2026: HOLD the explicit-content launch.** No route has cleared all three independent gates: Indian law, platform policy and payment/payout eligibility. This is a research decision, not a legal opinion. [Evidence and reasoning](docs/decision-report.md)

The proposed pivot removes explicit nudity/sexual acts and the external adult-subscription funnel from the intended product. It retains a disclosed, photorealistic adult AI persona and proposes non-nude glamour content through Instagram Subscriptions. This is a proposed scope change, not an approved business model or a revision of the original audit findings.

The transition document records a provisional objective of **₹20 lakh annualized gross revenue run rate within 12–18 months**, approximately **$10–20/month** operating budget, **24 hours/week** founder availability, and an initial **two-week** experimentation window. These are planning inputs from the conversation, not validated forecasts or authorization to begin experimentation.

## Proposed next verification stage

The [transition baseline](RESEARCH_SCOPE_TRANSITION.md) reserves these questions for a separate primary-source reassessment:

1. Indian creator access to Instagram Subscriptions, including disclosed AI-profile eligibility and Indian bank payouts.
2. Current regional, account, age and follower requirements.
3. Content and Partner Monetization Policy treatment of subscription-only, non-nude glamour/suggestive content.
4. Whether restrictions affect individual posts, account-wide monetization, or access to Subscriptions.

AI-profile labeling, recommendation eligibility, Indian legal/SGI boundaries, native payment architecture and tax/remittance treatment also require verification for the revised model. The conversation's August 2026 AI-profile policy claims and reach-penalty correction have **not been independently verified in this repository's evidence register**. Agreement between assistants is not primary-source confirmation.

If the native-only model is adopted, third-party adult-platform and direct-checkout investigations become lower priorities. Their original findings remain preserved; Stripe/Razorpay merchant terms should not be assumed to govern a different native subscription arrangement. Meta's actual collection and payout terms still need verification.

The research sequence remains: review the transition baseline, authorize the targeted reassessment, then decide further work. No new policy audit, persona creation, content generation or experiment is initiated by this README update.

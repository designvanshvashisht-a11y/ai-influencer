# AI Influencer Feasibility Audit

Preliminary legal, platform-policy and payment audit for an **India-based operator of a fictional, clearly adult AI influencer**, using Instagram lifestyle/suggestive content and proposed paid, explicitly disclosed AI adult subscriptions.

**As of 17 October 2026: HOLD the explicit-content launch.** No route has cleared all three independent gates: Indian law, platform policy and payment/payout eligibility. This is a research decision, not a legal opinion. [Evidence and reasoning](docs/decision-report.md)

## Reading the result

- **Confirmed:** cited policy/statutory text was located. This does not mean the operator is approved.
- **Interpretation:** application of that text to the proposed business.
- **Unknown:** sufficient current, applicable evidence or provider confirmation was not obtained.

Fanvue is an AI-policy candidate with India eligibility still unverified. Patreon and Fansly impose realism restrictions. OnlyFans primary verification is incomplete. Instagram promotion and payment acceptance require separate review. See the [comparison](docs/platform-comparison.md) and register for the supporting sources and qualifications.

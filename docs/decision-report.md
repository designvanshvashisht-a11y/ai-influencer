# Preliminary decision report

**2026-10-17 · Decision: HOLD the proposed paid explicit-content launch.** This is a feasibility recommendation, not a court ruling or an Indian legal opinion. No end-to-end route is confirmed. The [register](evidence-register.md) records the supporting primary texts, their dates, jurisdiction and limitations; the [comparison](platform-comparison.md) separates platform access from payments.

The operator resides/works in India; the character is fictional and clearly adult; Instagram would carry lifestyle/suggestive posts leading to explicitly disclosed AI adult subscriptions. Realistic and illustrated variants were evaluated separately. Subscriber countries, operator entity, receiving bank and exact content boundaries were not supplied and remain material unknowns. No persona, prompts, images, account, payment transaction or consumer dataset was created.

Priority: legislation/notified rules; provider contractual policies; official help/explanations; adjudicative records. Secondary material is explicitly marked. Retrieval failures, relative update dates, historical policy statements, old consolidations and jurisdiction mismatches are preserved. This was a targeted preliminary search, not an exhaustive case-law or amendment search.

## Interpretations, kept separate from statutory facts

**India legal gate: not cleared.** Applying the content-neutral statutory wording to fully synthetic explicit adults presents substantial criminal-law risk. The exact classification of particular media, exceptions and interaction between statutes require Indian counsel. We did not locate a controlling judgment resolving this precise business; that does not prove no such judgment exists. Foreign hosting, an adult paywall, AI disclosure or foreign customers are not established exemptions. These are interpretations from [L01-L08], not predictions of prosecution.

**Instagram growth gate: not cleared for the adult funnel.** Current exact solicitation text could not be fully retrieved. Historical Meta primary evidence supports a pornography-link restriction; recent records support continuing digital-content and disclosure rules. Neither proves every subscription-domain URL is banned. Link aggregators supply no documented permission. Claims about automatic link-scanning, guaranteed shadowbans or reliable reach were not substantiated. [M01-M05]

**Payment gate: not cleared independently of law.** No source verifies the exact chain of Indian operator, adult AI category, acquiring/collection contract, payout service and receiving bank. No prediction of automatic freezing or law-enforcement referral for every adult-platform remittance is justified. New 2026 RBI material was located through reproductions, so current original instruments and implementation must be obtained before giving operational remittance advice. [P03-P04, F01-F05]

**A non-explicit lifestyle business is a separate candidate for review, not a cleared pivot.** “Non-nude,” “glamour” and “suggestive” are not legal or payment safe harbors. Any changed scope needs a fresh review of actual boundaries, advertising, platform treatment and money flow. [L01-L08, M01-M05, F02]
